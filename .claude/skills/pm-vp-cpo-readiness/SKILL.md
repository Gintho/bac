# VP/CPO Readiness Advisor

## Purpose
Adaptive coaching for the Director → VP/CPO transition. Most Directors excel at Product but struggle with People — the dimension that most determines executive product success. This advisor diagnoses your specific situation and delivers targeted recommendations, not generic career advice.

**When to use:** Preparing for the leap, evaluating or interviewing, newly landed (first 6 months), or recalibrating if struggling in an established VP/CPO role.

## Key Concepts

**The Three Ps framework:**
- **Product:** Portfolio decisions, cross-product strategy, investment allocation across bets
- **Practice:** Process discipline, cross-functional execution, PM team health, quality standards
- **People:** Organizational design, talent assessment, leader development, succession planning

Most Directors underinvest in People. Most VP/CPO failures trace back to it.

**The Empowerment Myth:** Promotion doesn't simplify constraints — it multiplies them. Decision complexity grows exponentially: you're no longer saying "here's what we're building" but "here's how we invest across 5 product bets, 3 markets, and 2 acquisition candidates."

**The CEO Interview Framework (VP→CPO shift):**
The cognitive reframe: stop asking "What are we releasing?" and start asking "What business outcomes is the product organization accountable for?"

In executive forums, translate product language (features, sprints, velocity) into business language (ROI, retention impact, EBITDA contribution, CAC payback).

**Time horizon expansion:** Quarterly delivery stays non-negotiable. But CPO-level thinking runs 3–5 years forward simultaneously. You need two mental operating modes: operator (this quarter) and architect (3 years out).

## Application

### Step 1: Identify Your Transition Scenario

Ask: "Which scenario best describes where you are right now?"

1. **Preparing for the leap** — Still a Director, building toward VP/CPO over 6-18 months
2. **Evaluating or interviewing** — Actively in process for a VP/CPO role
3. **Newly landed** — First 6 months in a VP/CPO role
4. **Recalibrating** — Established in the role but facing specific challenges

### Step 2: Diagnose the Three Ps

For each scenario, ask a targeted diagnostic question per dimension:

**Product dimension:**
- "How many products/bets does the org currently manage, and who owns the investment allocation decisions?"
- Red flag: If the answer is "the CEO decides," that's a constraint you'll need to earn your way out of.

**Practice dimension:**
- "How healthy is the PM org's process discipline — roadmap fidelity, stakeholder communication, discovery cadence?"
- Red flag: If you inherited a PM team that ships features without discovery, your first 90 days will be triage.

**People dimension:**
- "Of your direct reports, how many could step into your prior role tomorrow? How many need 12 months of development?"
- Red flag: If you can't name a successor for your own role, you're not operating at VP/CPO level yet.

### Step 3: Deliver Scenario-Specific Recommendations

**Scenario 1 — Preparing for the Leap:**

```
Based on your Three Ps assessment, here are your priority development areas:

1. [People gap]: Start here — this is the hardest skill to develop quickly
   Action: Lead one cross-functional initiative where you don't control headcount
2. [Product gap]: ...
3. [Practice gap]: ...
4. Executive presence: Begin translating product updates into business impact language
   in your next 3 leadership presentations
5. Peer relationships: Identify your future CRO, CFO, CMO equivalents and build
   those relationships before you need them
```

**Scenario 2 — Evaluating or Interviewing:**

CEO interview framework — the questions you must answer confidently:
- "How would you structure the product investment portfolio for the next 18 months?"
- "How do you think about the tradeoff between platform and features?"
- "How do you hold the PM team accountable for outcomes vs. outputs?"
- "Walk me through how you'd diagnose the product org in your first 90 days"

Preparation exercise: For each question, write a 200-word answer grounded in specific past examples. Generic answers don't land at this level.

**Scenario 3 — Newly Landed (First 6 Months):**

30-60-90 framework:
- Days 1-30: Listen only. Interview every direct report, key cross-functional peer, 10 customers. Form no opinions publicly.
- Days 31-60: Synthesize and share back. Share your observations without prescriptions. Test your read of the org.
- Days 61-90: Make 2-3 visible decisions that demonstrate your values and priorities. These set the cultural norms.

Non-negotiable in first 90 days: Weekly 1:1s with CRO, CFO, CMO. "Without these alliances, you're a dead man walking." Proactively communicate trade-offs before announcements, not after.

**Scenario 4 — Recalibrating:**

Diagnostic: Which P is the failure point?
- Product: Are roadmap bets underperforming? → Audit investment thesis per bet
- Practice: Is the team missing commitments? → Inspect the discovery-to-delivery process
- People: High PM turnover or poor quality? → Org design and hiring bar audit

```
Based on your symptoms, here's a prioritized recalibration plan:

1. [Primary lever] — addresses [specific symptom]
2. [Secondary lever] — ...
3. [90-day diagnostic checkpoint] — how you'll know if it's working
```

### Step 4: Build the Alliance Map

For any scenario, map the five critical executive alliances:
- CRO: Shared revenue narrative
- CFO: Unit economics and investment justification fluency
- CMO: GTM sequencing and positioning alignment
- CTO/CPO peers: Technical feasibility and platform prioritization
- CEO: Strategic portfolio and board communication

For each: current relationship quality (1-5), frequency of proactive communication, last time you brought a trade-off to them before a decision was made.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Continuing to operate as a Director:** The most common VP/CPO failure is continuing to do the job you were good at. Let go of individual roadmap ownership.
- **Postponing People investment:** "I'll focus on talent after we ship X." There is no after. Team quality compounds or decays every quarter.
- **Skipping the listening phase:** New VP/CPOs who arrive with pre-formed opinions destroy trust in the first 30 days before they've earned it.
- **Speaking product language to the board:** If your board update is "we shipped 43 features," you've failed the translation test.
